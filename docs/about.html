I'm Heather; Graphic Designer in Tacoma, WA

I'm a fairly friendly, mildly sarcastic gal who strives to challenge the status quo and break away from the trends. I'm a think-outside-the-box-er. Boxes are only good for storage, and your visual identity isn't storage! Is this analogy helping?

What's neat about me is i'm a brand designer and web designer. I like to keep my work wacky and interesting. You get all the best with my prowess for typography, spacing, balance, purpose, blah blah... but also the advantages of someone who is real nerdy with UX, CTAs, and layout.

I graduated at the elderly age of 30 with my BFA in graphic design & media arts. I studied everything from graphic design, web design, illustration, game art, animation, and marketing. My favorite projects involve bringing clarity to a confused visual presence, providing solid footing to a website that was lost, and/or bringing energy to a project that lacked grit & ambition.

Simply put: I love brand strategy, web design, and elevating brand awareness so your company shines as it should.
