hi there!

I'm Heather; A freelance brand designer & photographer.

I graduated at the elderly age of 30 with my BFA in graphic design & media arts. I went to college online, ew. I studied everything from graphic design, web design, illustration, game art, animation, and marketing. I'm currently based in Tacoma, Washington, but I know how to operate a computer so I work with clients all over the map.

Now, I won't lie and say that I've had a camera in my hand since I was able to walk and I've been super artsy growing up. I actually grew up in a very dysfunctional home, and was always told, "You can't." After I left home at eighteen, I made it my mission to show everyone that I can.

Thus, my era of trying all of the things began. I became a flight attendant and traveled the world. I worked as a first responder. I started my degree program, not knowing if I was creative at all, and graduated in three years with my BFA. With a fire lit under my butt, I've been out here trying to paint the town in the boldest, most fun colors I can. I strive to bring as much emotion, joy, fun, and authenticity I can in all of my creative projects from photography, brand design, web design, marketing, etc.
So, welcome! Follow me on socials, reach out about a project, or just say hello.

Find me on Insta/Threads and/or THE Facebook.
