Graphic Designer + Photographer in Boise, ID

Hello all - I'm Heather! I'm a fairly friendly, mildly sarcastic gal with ADHD and a longing to think outside the box all the time.

I'm a designer and photographer. Design is an open-ended word and can mean many things, but my genres are branding and web design specifically. I love typography, spacing, balance, purpose, wireframing, UX, CTAs, layout, blah blah blah... but I'm also a nerd with lighting, composition, emotion, and stories. I love stories.

I graduated at the elderly age of 30 with my BFA in graphic design & media arts. I studied everything from graphic design, web design, illustration, game art, animation, and marketing. My favorite projects involve passionate business owners, wonderful families, milestone moments, and a story to tell.

I live just outside of Boise with my very loud family and when I'm not working, I'll be applying sunscreen and hiding from the sun in the shade, you'll hear us before you see us at a park, or we will be building a new lego set at home.
