I'm Heather; Designer for Brands & Web.

I graduated at the elderly age of 30 with my BFA in graphic design & media arts. I went to college online, ew. I studied everything from graphic design, web design, illustration, game art, animation, and marketing. I'm currently based in Tacoma, Washington... but I'm from Ohio and I recently moved here from Iowa.. after living in California... after living in Texas... after living in NYC, after living in Michigan.

I started as a flight attendant, I worked as a first responder, and then got my BFA, not knowing if I was creative at all. With a fire lit under my butt, I've been out here trying to paint the town in the boldest, most intentional colors I can. I strive to bring as much emotion, joy, fun, and authenticity I can in all of my creative projects from photography, brand design, web design, marketing, etc.
